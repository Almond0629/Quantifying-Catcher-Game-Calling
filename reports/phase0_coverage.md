# Phase 0 coverage report

Generated by `python3.12 -m gamecalling.phase0`. Denominator: all regular-season Statcast pitch rows.

Action unit: `pitch_type` (families are a sensitivity check). Arsenal rule: judged per batter hand on the two training folds (selection + valuation) of each scoring fold combined; the pitcher must pitch in both. SP: share >= 2% and n >= 10; RP: share >= 5% and n >= 5, against that hand. 3 game folds per season, seed 20261024. SP = at least 50% of the pitcher's appearances in the two training folds are starts.
